Stuffed Eggplant

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant
  • 150 grams of never frozen chicken breast
  • 1 tsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp mushroom stock
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 1 stalk spring onion, finely sliced

Preparation

  1. Slice the eggplant 1.5 to 2 centimeters thick
  2. Remove the center of the egglplant to make it hollow
  3. Chop the chicken into bite sized segments
  4. Add the cornstarch, flour, spring onion and seasoning into a bowl and mix
  5. Bake or air fry at 180C for 15 to 18 minutes
  6. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onions
