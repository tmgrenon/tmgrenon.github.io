Stir-Fried Siamese Pumpkin

Ingredients

  • 1 medium Siamese pumpkin (chayote), peeled and cut into thin matchsticks or cubes
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced or minced
  • 4 to 6 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 2 to 3 red or green chilies, sliced (optional for heat)
  • 1 tomato, chopped (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil for sautéing
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt (to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground white or black pepper
  • 100 ml (approx. 1/3 cup) water

Preparation

  1. Peel the chayote: Wear gloves or peel it under running water to avoid the sticky sap.
  2. Cut into matchsticks: Slice the chayote thin, then cut into matchstick-sized strips.
  3. Remove excess moisture: Toss the strips with 1 teaspoon of salt. Let sit for 10 minutes, then squeeze out the water and rinse.
  4. Sauté the aromatics: Heat 1 tablespoon of cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.
  5. Add garlic and shallots: Fry the garlic, shallots, and chilies for about 2 minutes until fragrant.
  6. Cook the chayote: Toss in the chayote matchsticks and stir well to coat with the aromatics.
  7. Season the dish: Pour in the oyster sauce, salt, sugar, white pepper, and water.
  8. Simmer until tender: Cook for 4 to 5 minutes until the chayote is crisp-tender and the liquid reduces.
