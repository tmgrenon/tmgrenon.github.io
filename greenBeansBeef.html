Stir-Fried Green Beans and Beef

Ingredients

  • 250 to 300 grams of green beans, cut into 4-5 cem pieces
  • 150 grams of tenderloin beef, thinly sliced
  • Three cloves of garlic, finely chopped
  • Two sprays of olive oil
  • 50 to 80 ml of water
  • One tbsp soy sauce
  • One tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1/2 tbsp sweet soy sauce
  • 1/4 tsp ground pepper
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 mushroom stock or beef stock (optional)
  • 1/4 tsp sugar (optional)
  • 1/4 tsp sesame oil (optional)

Preparation

  1. Heat the Oil and Saute the Garlic until fragrant
  2. Add beef and Stir-Fry
  3. Add the green beans
  4. Add all spices and water
  5. Add the beef
  6. Cook for three to five minutes, until the green beans are cooks through and crispy
  7. Adjust the seasoning and serve
