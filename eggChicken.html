Steamed Egg with Chopped Chicken

Ingredients

  • Two fresh eggs
  • 200 ml warm water
  • 150 grams minced chicken
  • Two cloves minced garlic
  • One tablespoon oyster sauce
  • One tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 30-50 ml water

Preparation

  1. Beat the eggs gently (avoid foaming)
  2. Stir in the warm water
  3. Skim the egg foam
  4. Cover the bowl with aluminum foil or a plate
  5. Steam over low heat for about 12-15 minutes until set
  6. Saute the garlic until fragrant
  7. Add chicken and spices
  8. Cook until the sauce thickens slightly
